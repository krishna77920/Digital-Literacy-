# Prevention Checklist: Keep Yourself Safe Online
## Overall Safety Advice:
-Make sure all of your accounts have strong, one-of-a-kind passwords, and turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).  
-Steer clear of downloading attachments from unidentified sources and clicking on dubious links.  
-Before disclosing any information, always confirm the legitimacy of emails, messages, and websites.  
-Update your devices with the most recent security patches and software.  
-Avoid posting too much personal information on social media, such as your address, phone number, and college information.  
-Avoid using public Wi-Fi to access sensitive accounts; instead, use secure networks.

## UPI and Financial Safety Advice:
-Never give out your OTP, UPI PIN, or banking information to anyone, even if they say they are from the bank.  
-Verify payment requests on apps such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm before approving them.  
-Before sending money, make sure the recipient's name and UPI ID are correct.
-Unknown QR codes should not be scanned since they could result in fraudulent transactions.

## Cybercrime Reporting:
-Use the National Cyber Crime Portal to report instances of cybercrime: Cybercrime.gov.in. 
-For prompt assistance, contact the Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930.
