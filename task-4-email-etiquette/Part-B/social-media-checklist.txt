## Do’s
- Be respectful and keep it professional in your posts and comments.  
- Make sure your profile info is accurate and appropriate.  
- Share content that’s useful, informative, or at least positive.  
- Check your privacy settings to keep your personal info safe.  
- Think a bit before you post... consider how it might look down the road.  

## Don’ts
- Don’t share sensitive personal stuff like your address or phone number.  
- Avoid posting anything offensive, abusive, or just plain inappropriate.  
- Steer clear of online arguments or toxic debates.  
- Don’t spread misinformation or news that’s not verified.  
- Keep your digital reputation intact—don’t act unprofessionally.
